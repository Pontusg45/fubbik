# Favorites and Pins

## Favorites

Click the star icon on any chunk to favorite it. Favorites are stored server-side and appear on your dashboard under "Favorites."

- Favorites sync across devices (they're tied to your user account)
- The dashboard shows your favorited chunks with quick-access links
- Use favorites for chunks you reference frequently

## Pinned Chunks

Pin chunks locally for quick access during a work session. Pinned chunks are stored in localStorage and don't sync across devices.

- Pins are temporary — useful for "working set" access
- Pinned chunks appear in the sidebar
- Clear pins when you're done with a task

## Recently Viewed

Your dashboard also shows "Recently Viewed" chunks with timestamps, making it easy to return to chunks you were just looking at.
